The guided steps

Eight steps, one question at a time

The first three are a conversation. After that Claude builds, measures and checks on its own, then comes back with screenshots.

You what the designer does Claude what Claude does

  1. Name and words

    You

    Share the title, headline and body copy, or a screenshot.

    Claude

    Reads the copy, confirms it and suggests a short name for the link.

  2. Files

    You

    Attach the background, the boxes, the option art and the mock-ups.

    Claude

    Makes a contact sheet, says which file is which and flags anything missing.

  3. Questions and options

    You

    Give each card’s text and options, and pick an animation style.

    Claude

    Matches options to art and recommends a style for each question.

  4. Build

    Claude

    Creates the scene’s folder and page, and fills in its settings file.

  5. Place the art

    Claude

    Finds each illustration in your mock-ups to get its exact position and size.

  6. Layer order

    Claude

    Works out what sits in front of what, like the doctor standing behind the bed but in front of the drip.

  7. Check

    Claude

    Screenshots every state, compares them with your mock-ups, captures the animations and checks for broken files.

  8. Share

    Claude

    Sends the screenshots, publishes the scene and gives you the link.

    You

    Ask for changes in plain words.

Behind the scenes

How Claude sees, measures and checks

The skill comes with four small scripts. These are real outputs from the hospitalisation scene.

Sees every file

Claude can’t open an SVG by eye, so it renders all your uploads into one labelled sheet with their sizes. That’s how it tells the doctor box from the ward box before asking you to confirm.

Measures from your mock-ups

Each illustration is searched for in every mock-up, at the scene’s scale and at the mock-up’s scale. Art drawn smaller still lands in the right place.

Good to know

Four things that save a round trip

Export mock-ups full frame

The whole scene at any size, never a crop. Measuring relies on it, and Claude warns you if a mock-up looks cropped.

Show every option somewhere

Art that appears in no mock-up gets placed by hand next to its box. It works, but a mock-up makes it exact.

Filenames are case-sensitive

On the live site Armchair.svg and armchair.svg are different files. Claude keeps your names and checks they match.

The repository is public

Anything uploaded, including mock-ups, can be seen by anyone. Keep confidential work out, or make the repository private.
